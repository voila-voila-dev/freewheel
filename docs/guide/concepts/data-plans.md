# Data plans

Each SIM card has its own counter, remembered even if you move the card to another slot.

**You tell freewheel** (operators don't share it):
- how much data the plan includes, for example 25 GB, or unlimited;
- the day it renews each month, or that it's a one-off pack;
- when to warn you, for example at 50 %, 80 % and 100 %;
- the price beyond the cap, if any.

**freewheel shows you** what you used this period, what's left, and what going over would cost.
The full history is always kept.

**Rule of thumb.** One hour of live at 2.5 Mbps uses about 1.1 GB.
