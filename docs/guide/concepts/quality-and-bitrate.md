# Quality and bitrate

**Bitrate** is how much video you send per second, in megabits per second (**Mbps**).
More Mbps means a sharper picture, and more data used.

| Bitrate | Looks like | Data per hour |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Mbps | watchable, soft | ~0.45 GB |
| 2.5 Mbps | good 720p | ~1.1 GB |
| 6 Mbps | sharp 1080p | ~2.7 GB |

**What freewheel does for you.** It lowers the bitrate when the network is weak and raises it when
it recovers, so the stream keeps going instead of freezing. Sound is always protected first:
viewers forgive a soft picture, not choppy audio.
