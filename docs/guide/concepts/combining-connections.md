# Combining connections

A phone uses one network at a time. freewheel uses **all of them at once**: two or three SIM cards,
a café's Wi-Fi, your phone's hotspot.

**Why it matters.** Each network has bad moments: a hill, a crowded cell, a tunnel. They rarely
have them at the same time. Sending your video over several of them is like taking several roads
at once: if one is blocked, the others still get through.

**What freewheel does for you.**
- It measures every connection many times per second.
- It sends each piece of video on the connection where it will arrive first.
- When a connection dies, the others take over before your viewers notice.

**Tip.** SIMs from **different operators** work best: two SIMs on the same mast tend to fail together.
