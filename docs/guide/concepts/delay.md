# Delay

Your viewers see you **a few seconds after** things happen. That delay has three parts:

1. **The safety margin** freewheel keeps so it can repair lost pieces of video (under a second to two seconds).
2. **Your server** preparing the stream.
3. **YouTube or Twitch**, which add several seconds of their own.

A bigger safety margin means fewer glitches; a smaller one means faster replies to your chat.
You choose the balance in your settings.
