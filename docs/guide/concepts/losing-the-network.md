# Losing the network

On the road, the network **will** drop: tunnels, mountains, the desert. That's part of the adventure.

**What your viewers see** depends on your setting:
- **Scene**: an animation, games (bet on where you'll reappear!), your best moments.
- **Raw**: a few seconds of the real cut, then the "I'm back!" moment.
- **Mixed**: raw for a few seconds, then the scene if it lasts.

**What freewheel does for you.**
- Your camera keeps recording: nothing is lost.
- Your session stays open for about 15 minutes: when any network comes back, the live resumes
  where it was, without starting over.
- Your position is kept and sent when the network returns.
