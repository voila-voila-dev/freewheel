# Your full-quality recording

freewheel makes **two videos at once**:
- **the live**, light, adapted to the network, sent right away;
- **the recording**, in full quality, kept on the box.

The recording is cut into small numbered pieces. When there is spare network (or Wi-Fi in the
evening), the box sends the pieces your server doesn't have yet, each one once. You can follow
progress on your phone.

**Is everything uploaded by the evening?** Not always over 4G alone: the full-quality video is about
three times heavier than the live. What isn't sent yet stays on the box and goes up on the next
Wi-Fi. You can delete videos from the box **only once your server has confirmed it has them**,
by hand or automatically.

**It's optional.** Turn it off, or limit it to Wi-Fi, to save data.
