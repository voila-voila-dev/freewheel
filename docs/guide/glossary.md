# Glossary

- **Bitrate**: how much video is sent per second, in Mbps. More is sharper and uses more data.
- **Bonding**: combining several connections into one, so they back each other up.
- **Box**: the small computer that turns your camera's image into a live stream.
- **Delay (latency)**: the time between something happening and your viewers seeing it.
- **Handover**: your phone network switching from one mast to the next as you move.
- **Mbps**: megabits per second. 8 megabits = 1 megabyte.
- **Outage**: a moment with no network at all.
- **Packet**: a small piece of your video, about 1,200 bytes, sent on its own.
- **Repair packet**: extra data that lets the server rebuild a lost packet without asking again.
- **Server**: your own computer on the internet that receives your stream and sends it to YouTube or Twitch.
- **SIM / ICCID**: your SIM card, and the number printed on it that identifies it.
